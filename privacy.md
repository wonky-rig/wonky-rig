# Privacy Policy for Wonky Rig

**Last updated: October 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use **Wonky Rig**, including during development, testing, and online multiplayer gameplay.

## 1. Information We Use

Wonky Rig may use Epic Online Services (EOS) and Epic Account Services to provide online features.

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, the game may access information associated with your Epic Games account, including:

- Epic Account ID or other online service identifiers;
- Epic Games display name;
- basic profile information;
- friends list;
- online presence and availability;
- lobby, party, session, and multiplayer participation information;
- technical information required to establish and maintain online connections.

Wonky Rig requests only the information required to provide its current online features.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

- authenticate players;
- identify players in online sessions;
- display player names;
- provide friends and social features;
- send and receive game or lobby invitations;
- create and manage parties and multiplayer lobbies;
- connect players to multiplayer game sessions and dedicated servers;
- maintain multiplayer synchronization and gameplay functionality;
- diagnose technical problems during development and testing.

## 3. Epic Online Services

Wonky Rig uses **Epic Online Services** and may use **Epic Account Services**.

Epic Games may process information independently in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

You can review Epic Games' privacy information on the official Epic Games website.

Wonky Rig does not control how Epic Games processes information within Epic's own services.

## 4. Local Game Data

Wonky Rig may store certain game settings and profile information locally on your device, such as:

- game settings;
- control preferences;
- player profile settings;
- selected player name;
- other locally saved game preferences.

This information is primarily stored on the player's own device unless a future online feature explicitly requires server-side storage.

## 5. Multiplayer and Server Data

When participating in multiplayer sessions, temporary information necessary for gameplay may be processed by the game server.

This may include:

- player identifiers;
- player names;
- lobby and team information;
- session information;
- gameplay state;
- connection information;
- technical server logs used to diagnose problems.

During development and testing, technical logs may be reviewed to identify crashes, networking problems, synchronization issues, or other technical errors.

## 6. Analytics, Advertising, and Payments

The current version of Wonky Rig does **not** use third-party advertising.

The current development version does **not** operate its own advertising tracking system.

The current development version does **not** directly collect payment card information.

If analytics, advertising, payment services, or additional third-party services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those services are used publicly.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed or shared only when necessary to operate the game and its online services, including with service providers such as Epic Games through Epic Online Services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

## 8. Data Retention

Locally stored settings and profile information may remain on your device until you modify or delete the relevant game data.

Temporary multiplayer and server information may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, diagnose technical problems, maintain security, or comply with legal requirements.

Third-party services such as Epic Games apply their own retention policies to information processed through their systems.

## 9. Your Choices

Depending on the services used, you may be able to:

- decline requested Epic Account permissions;
- manage permissions through your Epic Games account;
- remove locally stored game data;
- stop using online multiplayer features.

Some online features may not function if the permissions required to provide them are declined.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Wonky Rig is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children except as permitted through the platform and online services used by the game.

Age-related requirements and parental controls may also be handled by Epic Games, Steam, or other platforms through which the game is distributed.

This policy may be updated before public release to reflect the final age rating and platform requirements.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may change as Wonky Rig develops and new features or services are introduced.

The date at the top of this page will be updated whenever material changes are made.
