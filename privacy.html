Privacy Policy for Wonky Rig

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Information We Use

Wonky Rig may use limited information necessary to provide online and multiplayer functionality, including:

  • Epic Account ID and other online identifiers;
  • Epic display name;
  • Basic profile information;
  • Friends list information;
  • Presence information;
  • Lobby, party, session, and multiplayer participation information;
  • Technical connection information required for online services.

2. How We Use Information

This information may be used to:

  • authenticate players;
  • identify players in online services;
  • display player names;
  • provide friends and social features;
  • send and receive invitations;
  • create and join parties and lobbies;
  • connect players to multiplayer sessions and dedicated servers;
  • synchronize multiplayer gameplay;
  • diagnose technical or connection problems.

3. Epic Online Services

Wonky Rig uses Epic Online Services and Epic Account Services for some online functionality.

Epic Games may independently process information associated with Epic accounts and Epic Online Services according to its own terms, privacy policies, and data practices.

4. Local Game Data

Wonky Rig may store certain information locally on your device, including:

  • game settings;
  • control settings and key bindings;
  • local profile information;
  • selected player name;
  • other configuration data required for the game to function.

5. Multiplayer and Server Data

During online multiplayer sessions, information may be temporarily processed by multiplayer servers, including:

  • player identifiers;
  • display names;
  • party, lobby, team, and session information;
  • gameplay synchronization data;
  • connection information;
  • technical server logs used for diagnostics and security.

6. Analytics, Advertising, and Payments

The current version of Wonky Rig does not include third-party advertising, does not operate its own advertising tracking system, and does not directly collect payment card information.

If analytics, advertising, payment services, or other data-processing features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary with service providers used to operate the game and its online functionality, or when required by law.

8. Data Retention

Local game data may remain on your device until it is modified or removed.

Server-side information may be retained temporarily for the operation, security, maintenance, and troubleshooting of multiplayer services.

Information processed by third-party service providers is subject to their own retention policies.

9. Your Choices

Depending on the services you use, you may be able to:

  • decline optional Epic account permissions;
  • manage permissions through your Epic account;
  • remove locally stored game data;
  • stop using online or multiplayer features.

10. Children's Privacy

Wonky Rig is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is permitted and handled by the relevant platform or online service.

Age requirements, parental controls, and account restrictions provided by platforms such as Epic Games or Steam may apply.

This Privacy Policy may be updated before public release to reflect applicable age-related requirements and platform policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Wonky Rig develops and new features, online services, or data practices are introduced.

The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
